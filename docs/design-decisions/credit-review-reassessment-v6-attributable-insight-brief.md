# Credit review reassessment V6 — attributable insight brief

Status: archived

Historical outcome: V6 established a calmer single-brief direction and reduced repeated assessment language. V7 subsequently restored a stronger decision hierarchy, V8 established the evidence-first editorial workflow, and V9 is now current; V6 remains preserved as a selectable design-history option.

Mercury reference: focused expense review and Treasury account surfaces, audited July 27, 2026.

## Decision

Make the finding detail a single insight brief when the analyst opens a current reassessment. The brief leads with the verified change, the residual exposure, and one system conclusion. The evidence basis remains progressively disclosed below, and the sticky footer owns the one accountable analyst action: record judgment.

## Why

The previous V5 direction was structurally sound but still repeated the same conclusion across a risk card, a change summary, and an evidence panel. Mercury's calmer review tasks use one readable object followed by a small number of supporting rows. V6 keeps the important distinctions—system conclusion, changed evidence, unchanged exposure, and human judgment—without making each distinction a separate raised card.

## Interaction contract

- `Updated assessment` is attributable product language; it does not imply that a model made the decision.
- `System conclusion` remains read-only and is never overwritten by analyst judgment.
- `Evidence changed` and `Still true` are sibling ledger rows, not competing CTAs.
- `Assessment basis` stays collapsed by default and preserves provenance details.
- `Record judgment` remains the only primary action on the durable finding page.
- When V6 was current, V1–V5 remained available in Design Tools. V6 itself remains preserved after the V7 and V8 promotions.

## Responsive contract

At narrow widths the brief stacks its signal rows and evidence ledger, keeps the title and status readable, and lets the judgment footer return to document flow. No horizontal scroll is introduced.
